How It Works

Reporting a campus issue takes only a few steps.

1

Create Account

Register as a student using your name, email and department.

2

Report Issue

Select the issue category, department and location and submit the problem.

3

Authority Handles It

The responsible department assigns and works on the issue.

4

Track Resolution

Follow the status and updates until the issue is resolved.
